# UN Sustainable Development Goals (reference)

| # | Name | Short focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No Poverty | End poverty in all its forms |
| 2 | Zero Hunger | Food security, nutrition, sustainable agriculture |
| 3 | Good Health and Well-being | Healthy lives and well-being at all ages |
| 4 | Quality Education | Inclusive, equitable education and lifelong learning |
| 5 | Gender Equality | Gender equality, empower women and girls |
| 6 | Clean Water and Sanitation | Water and sanitation for all |
| 7 | Affordable and Clean Energy | Affordable, reliable, sustainable, modern energy |
| 8 | Decent Work and Economic Growth | Inclusive growth, productive employment |
| 9 | Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure | Resilient infrastructure, innovation |
| 10 | Reduced Inequalities | Reduce inequality within and among countries |
| 11 | Sustainable Cities and Communities | Inclusive, safe, resilient, sustainable cities |
| 12 | Responsible Consumption and Production | Sustainable consumption and production |
| 13 | Climate Action | Combat climate change and its impacts |
| 14 | Life Below Water | Oceans, seas, marine resources |
| 15 | Life on Land | Ecosystems, forests, desertification, biodiversity |
| 16 | Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions | Peaceful, inclusive societies, accountable institutions |
| 17 | Partnerships for the Goals | Global partnership for sustainable development |
